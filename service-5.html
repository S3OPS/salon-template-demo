Extensions & Length — Length and Fullness in Austin

For clients who want length or fullness their own hair won't give them yet. This page sells trust: an honest consultation, quality hair, and a written plan — with no obligation to book.

The Matching Consultation

What we look at during the consultation and what the client learns from it.

  • A look at your whole head of hair, not just the length
  • Color and texture matching, in natural light
  • Our honest read on whether your hair can take it
  • Answers to every question, in plain language

Your Written Plan

What the plan includes, so it is easy to compare and approve.

  • Hair, install, and blending priced line by line
  • Methods at more than one budget level
  • Move-up costs and timing, stated clearly
  • Your quote held for a stated number of days

No-Obligation Honesty

Our promise about how the consultation feels — helpful, never pushy.

  • We recommend only what your hair actually needs
  • Honest alternatives if extensions are not right yet
  • No pressure to decide on the spot
  • The plan is yours to keep and compare

What You'll Receive

  • A complete assessment of your hair
  • A written, line-by-line plan and price
  • Options explained at different budgets
  • A clear recommendation — and the reasoning behind it
  • Follow-up answers by phone or email at no charge

Note: Say here whether the consultation is free, or costs a fee that is credited toward your install. Either way, say it plainly — clients respect plain.
