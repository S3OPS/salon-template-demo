Blowout & Finish — The Polished Finish in Austin

The finishing service — blowouts, silk presses, and styled looks for big days and ordinary ones. Explain the experience: how long it takes, how long it lasts, and who it is for.

Book an Appointment View All Services

How It Goes

How the appointment flows, so clients know exactly what to expect.

  • Wash and deep-condition first
  • Heat protection on every strand
  • Blowout, silk press, or styled finish
  • Finishing products matched to your hair

For Every Hair Type

How the finish adapts — because no two heads of hair behave the same.

  • Natural hair and silk presses
  • Fine hair that needs body, not weight
  • Thick hair that needs time and patience
  • Curly hair, styled or defined

Make It Last

How to keep the finish alive for days after you leave.

  • Wrap or pineapple it at night — we'll show you how
  • Dry shampoo at the roots, never the ends
  • A quick refresh booking when you need one
  • Honest guidance on heat, by phone, free

What You'll Receive

  • Your finished style, smooth and polished
  • A note on what your hair responded to best
  • Product recommendations that fit your budget
  • Priority booking for your next finish
  • Zero commitment — book when you want it

Note: Book one finish at a time, or ask about a standing weekly appointment. No packages to prepay and nothing to lock into — clients come back because their hair looks good, not because they have to.
